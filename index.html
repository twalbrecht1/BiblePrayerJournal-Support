Common Questions

Using your journal

Version 1.2 imports NIV, NLT, and NASB 1995 through the app’s shared service. Choose your Bible version and import a passage—no provider account or API key is needed. Update the app if you still see the earlier connection screen.

How do I get started?

From version 1.1, first let iCloud check for an existing profile from your other device. If one arrives, it opens automatically. If this is your first journal, create a profile with your name, choose a Bible version, and select your activities. You can also choose Create a new profile while iCloud is checking if you want a separate space. Profiles do not require an account or password. Use Settings → Edit name & activities to change your choices, or Add profile to create another space.

Can I turn journal areas off without losing history?

Yes. Open Settings → Journal areas and use the Bible Journal, Prayer Journal, and Memory Verses switches. Turning an area off hides its shortcuts, entries, search results, and history from Today, Journal, Review, and topic browsing. Your writing, favorites, prayer answers, and memory practice history stay saved; turn the area back on to see them again. All three may be off at once. These choices apply to the current profile and sync with its settings. Journal exports include the saved entries from areas that are off.

Can I hide Verse of the Day separately?

Yes. Open Settings → Today and turn Verse of the Day on or off. This only shows or hides the daily passage on Today; your journal areas and saved entries stay unchanged. The choice belongs to the current profile and syncs with its settings.

Can I add more than one reading each day?

Yes. On Journal, select Daily Bible Journal and tap New entry. When only one journal area is enabled, its entries open automatically and the Activity selector is hidden. With multiple areas and All entries selected, choose a Bible reading, prayer, or memory verse from the New entry menu. On Today, the Daily Bible Journal card opens its journal area, where you can add a reading. Each entry can have its own passage, verse text, thoughts, topics, and date. Change the date when recording an earlier reading.

How do I import Bible verses?

Enter a passage such as John 3:16–17 or Psalm 23, select a version, and tap Import verses. WEB and KJV work without an API account. Version 1.2 also supports NIV, NLT, and NASB 1995 through the app’s shared service, with no provider account or API key needed by users. Import one chapter or a same-chapter verse range, up to 100 verses. Imports require an internet connection; you can also enter verse text manually.

Do I need an API.Bible account or key?

No. Version 1.2 removes the personal API-key field and account signup link. The app owner manages one shared API.Bible connection for NIV, NLT, and NASB 1995; users only choose a version. These imports are live. If you still see Bible Connection asking for a key, update the app to version 1.2. WEB and KJV work without a key in either version.

Will imported passages and my notes work offline?

Your own reflections, prayers, topics, and memory practice history remain saved and available offline. In version 1.2, new licensed imports save references and editions with your writing. NIV, NLT, and NASB text uses a temporary device cache, refreshed within 30 days; an expired or uncached passage needs an internet connection. Sharing and journal exports include your writing and licensed references rather than copies of licensed Bible text. Manually entered and public-domain text keeps its previous behavior.

What happens if the free Bible allowance is reached?

The shared connection uses API.Bible’s free, noncommercial Starter allowance across the app. If the allowance is reached or a licensed translation is unavailable, the app explains the problem and keeps your draft. WEB, KJV, and manual entry remain available. A requested translation is never silently replaced with another version.

Can I import ESV?

ESV text can currently be entered manually. API.Bible does not offer ESV, and a separate Crossway import connection is not yet included. See API.Bible’s translation guidance for current availability.

Are topics assigned automatically?

No. Grace, Truth, Mercy, and the other starter topics are choices for you to select. Only the topics you choose are saved on an entry. Importing a passage does not analyze it or add topics.

How do I review or edit topics?

Open Review → Manage topics or Settings → Topics. Tap a topic to see its entries, use the plus to add one, or open its menu to rename, merge, or remove it. Renaming updates matching tags in that profile; removing a topic keeps your writing. To merge, rename it to an existing topic.

How do I find an older reflection?

Use Review to search verse references, manually entered or public-domain Scripture, thoughts, prayers, and topics. Licensed text displayed from the temporary cache is not part of the permanent journal search index. Combine the activity, topics, date, and favorites filters. Open Topics to select one or more topics; entries matching any selected topic are shown. Clear the selection for all topics. Review topic chips also let you select several topics, and browsing from Manage topics offers the same checklist. Topic chips show entry counts. Change the entry order in Settings or with the sort button.

How do I practice a memory verse?

Open a memory entry and tap Practice this verse. Hide or reveal the passage, write what you remember, and record the practice. For a version 1.2 licensed import, load the passage online or from its unexpired device cache before recording the practice. An entry’s menu can also copy a Bible reading into Memory Verses with its selected topics.

How do I record an answered prayer?

Open the prayer and tap Record an answer, add your answer notes and the date, then save. You can also edit the prayer’s status to Praying, Answered, or Archived and filter by status in Review.

What appears in Today’s Activities?

Today’s Activities shows Bible journal entries dated today, new prayers entered today, Prayer Pages used for recorded prayer time, individual prayers marked prayed, and memory verses with a practice recorded today. Tap an activity to open its entry or page. Page and verse activities appear once for the local day; a page activity records prayer time and does not mean every prayer was completed. Activities from disabled journal areas stay hidden until you re-enable the area.

How do I create a Prayer Page?

On Today, tap Prayer Journal to open its area in the Journal tab. The area has New prayer, Create prayer page, top-level Prayer Pages, and an Individual Prayers heading above the dated prayer list. Pages included as subpages anywhere in this profile are hidden from the top level by default. To also show a subpage there, expand a containing page and choose Show on main page from the subpage’s … menu, or enable the same switch in Edit Prayer Page. Show only as subpage reverses this preference while keeping all inclusion links. Links from archived pages also count, and archived pages still follow the archive visibility option. Use Settings → Prayer Pages to manage the full list. Pages start collapsed with a summary such as Daily (Family → Owen, Tyler). Expand a row to browse its subpages. The menu beside the Prayer Pages heading shows or hides archived pages. Create a named page with an optional description, individual prayers, and links to other pages. The description appears centered below the page title. The top-right pencil opens the page editor. A page may also start empty to hold subpages. The editor’s Add all prayers from a Topic action opens a popup for one or more topics. Add prayers adds their currently matching prayers once each; save the page to keep the selection. Later prayers or topic changes do not add or remove prayers automatically. Prayer Pages are also available in Review and Settings, and an existing topic’s menu can create a page for that topic.

Can I filter Prayer Pages by several topics?

Yes. In Journal with Prayer Journal selected, open Filters → Topics and choose one or more topics. A prayer matches when it has any selected topic. The same search, date, favorites, and prayer-status filters also apply to the Prayer Pages section. A page stays visible if it or any included subpage has a matching prayer. Empty branches disappear, and collapsed summaries show matching subpages. Open a page to see only matching prayers in its tree; its active-prayer session uses the same filters. Each page also has a Topics checklist, a filter summary, and Clear filters. Clearing filters restores the full view without changing prayer selections, subpage links, or history. Filters are temporary display choices on that device.

How do I reorder Prayer Pages or drag a page into another page?

In Prayer Journal, tap Organize Prayer Pages. Under Order pages, choose Main pages or Subpages of … in Arrange, then use the handles on the right to reorder that list. The arrow beside a page opens its subpage list. Changes save automatically; the organizer shows all active pages in the selected list, regardless of Journal filters. Each containing page keeps its own subpage order, and reordering the main list preserves the order inside pages. To add a subpage, switch to Add subpages, hold a page card and drop it onto another page. This adds an inclusion link while keeping the page’s other placements, prayers, and history. Circular links, links across profiles, and archived pages are rejected. A page marked Show on main page remains visible at the top level; choose Show only as subpage from its … menu to hide that extra placement. The existing Add subpage picker is also available. Inside a page, Organize page still arranges its prayers and subpages together.

How do I move or consolidate prayers onto another page?

Open a page’s … menu and choose Move prayers to another page, or use that action inside an open page. Select a destination and tap Move prayers. By default, only prayers selected directly on the source page move. Turn on Include prayers from subpages to also remove their selections from active nested pages. Shared subpages change everywhere they appear, so the move screen explains this before you move. Page links, page notes, prayer writing, answers, and history remain in place. A direct-only move can leave a prayer visible on the source if a subpage also selects it.

The move includes every prayer status and ignores temporary Journal filters. Archived subpages stay unchanged. The destination must be an active page in the same profile, outside the source’s subpages. Its existing order is preserved and moved prayers are appended in source order. The same saved prayer already there is kept once; different saved prayers with matching wording stay separate. Cancel closes the screen without changes.

After a move leaves the source page with no prayers, the app asks Delete empty prayer page? Choose Keep page to leave it available, or Delete page to remove it from every containing page along with its page notes and recorded page prayer times. Individual prayers, their history, and independent subpages are kept. Subpages with no other containing pages return to the main list, subject to archive visibility. The app checks the whole page, including archived subpages, and keeps pages with remaining prayers or unavailable items still arriving from iCloud. It checks again before deletion in case the contents changed. Closing the prompt also keeps the page.

What does each editing control change?

The top-right pencil edits the page’s name, optional description, included subpages, and individual prayers. Add all prayers from a Topic opens a separate topic-selection popup and adds current matches as a one-time selection. Organize page only changes the order of prayers and subpages. Open an individual prayer and use Edit prayer to change its wording, topics, status, or selected pages. Add page note and Edit note change dated reflections about the page. Removing a subpage link keeps the independent page and its links elsewhere.

How do I build Daily, Family, Church, and Work pages?

Create Daily, Family, Church, Work, Owen, and Tyler as independent pages. On Daily, use Add subpage to select Family; on Family, include Owen and Tyler. Family shows its own direct prayers together with the prayers on Owen and Tyler, including their nested subpages. Each included subpage uses its own prayer selections; direct-prayer exclusions on Family do not hide Owen’s prayers. The same Family page can also be included on another page without moving or copying it. Create new page creates an independent page and includes it on the page you opened. In the Prayer Journal main list, a page included as a subpage appears beneath its containing pages; enable Show on main page to also list it at the top level. Expand a row to reach its subpages, or open a page to see its outline. Removing its last inclusion link returns it to the top level. Settings and Review → Prayer Pages continue to show every page for management. In the pencil editor’s Subpages section, turn a page on to include it and its prayers here, including nested subpages. Turn it off to remove only its link from this page. Tap Save to apply the changes; the subpage, its prayers, and its other placements remain saved. The app prevents circular links. Archiving a page hides that page wherever it is included while its subpages remain independently available.

Can the same prayer appear on several pages?

Yes. Several pages can select the same prayer or include the same subpage. It remains one saved prayer, with shared writing, answers, dated updates, and a shared Prayed today indicator. In the prayer editor, Prayer pages lets you choose one or more pages. New prayer on a page starts with that page selected. Add existing prayer includes it without changing its tags. Topic imports add current matches once; future prayers are not added automatically. In Edit Prayer Page, Direct prayers switches select prayers directly on that page. Turning one off removes its direct selection; it still appears if an included subpage selects it. The editor labels prayers included by subpages. Edit that subpage to change its prayers, or remove its inclusion link from this page.

How do I choose a prayer’s pages from the prayer itself?

Open the prayer and tap Prayer pages, or select Prayer pages from its … menu. Search for page names and turn on one or more pages to select this prayer directly on them. Turn a page off to remove its direct selection. Tap Save to apply the changed switches; Cancel leaves saved selections unchanged. This action keeps the prayer’s writing, topics, answers and history intact.

A page labelled Included through subpages can still show the prayer when its direct switch is off. Change the selection on the relevant subpage to remove it there. The checklist previews these inherited placements as you change switches. Existing archived placements are listed for reference; restore an archived page before changing its selection. Other pages and their subpage links keep their saved settings.

How do I keep a daily prayer history?

From the Prayer Journal tree, open any page’s … menu and choose Pray through this page. You can also use that action inside an open Prayer Page. The session visits active prayers on that page and every nested subpage, in page order, and lets you mark them prayed today. Current journal filters apply. The menu action is disabled for archived pages or when no matching active prayers are available. A prayer appearing in several subpages is visited once per session. Daily tracking is shared across pages and changes with your local calendar day. Use Add page note for a dated reflection about the whole page. On an individual prayer, use Mark prayed today or Add dated update, then expand History to revisit prayer times and updates. Tap a saved note to edit it. You may keep several notes on the same date, and pages initially show all prayer statuses, including answered and archived prayers. Prayer sessions use active prayers only. When a prayer’s title and My prayer text match apart from spacing, the text appears once wherever prayers are listed, including Prayer Page outlines and prayer selection lists, as well as in prayer sessions and prayer details. The saved text remains intact; longer or different prayer text is still shown.

How do I manage or hide Prayer Pages?

The pencil edits the name, optional description, subpage links, and individual prayer selections. Organize page changes the order of prayers and included pages. Edit on the list reorders its pages. The Prayer Journal main list omits subpages unless Show on main page is enabled in their menu or editor. Pages without containing pages appear automatically. The preference syncs through private iCloud and is included in exports. Settings and Review → Prayer Pages provide the complete management list. Archive or restore a page from its menu; the menu beside the Prayer Pages heading shows or hides archived pages. Archiving preserves its notes and prayers, and its subpages remain independently available. Removing a topic does not remove prayers already added through a one-time topic import. Turning Prayer Journal off hides its pages and history without deleting them. Pages, descriptions, links, notes, and prayer times sync through private iCloud and are included in profile exports.

Does my writing sync between devices?

From version 1.1, the app syncs profiles, topics, settings, readings, prayers, and memory verses through your private iCloud account. Install the updated app on both devices, use the same Apple Account with iCloud enabled, and keep both apps open and connected for the first transfer. Settings → iCloud sync shows the account state and successful upload/download times. Saved entries still work offline. Use Settings → Export this profile’s journal to save a JSON backup to Files. An import/restore feature is not currently included.

Why is my iPhone profile missing on iPad?

Earlier versions stored writing only on each device. Update both devices to the latest version without uninstalling the iPhone app. Check that both use the same Apple Account and have iCloud enabled, then allow time for the iPhone to upload and the iPad to download. Use the same app release on both devices. If an account or transfer fails, the app shows the problem instead of assuming your journal is empty.

What if I already created a second profile on iPad?

Both profiles are kept, even if their names match. After syncing, use Settings → Profile management to select the original iPhone profile. Profiles are identified separately and are not merged by name. Keep any writing in the other profile until you have saved or copied what you need.

What happens when I delete a profile?

Deleting a profile removes its writing from this device and iCloud, including entries in areas that are off and other synced devices once they connect, and requires typing its name to confirm. For older versions, the app also attempts to remove an unused personal API key stored on the device where you delete the profile. Export any writing you want to keep before deleting a profile. Profiles organize entries but do not lock them with a password.
