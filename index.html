Common Questions

Using your journal

How do I get started?

Create a profile with your name, choose a Bible version, and select your activities. Profiles do not require an account or password. Use Settings → Edit name & activities to change your choices, or Add profile to create another space.

Can I add more than one reading each day?

Yes. Tap the plus beside Daily Bible Journal on Today. Each entry can have its own passage, verse text, thoughts, topics, and date. Change the date when recording an earlier reading.

How do I import Bible verses?

Enter a passage such as John 3:16–17 or Psalm 23, select a version, and tap Import verses. WEB and KJV work without an API account. Imports require an internet connection; you can also enter verse text manually.

Why does Bible Connection ask for an API key?

The current app uses manual API.Bible setup for NLT and NIV. Enter your key under Settings → Bible Connection and tap Save key & find translations. Access depends on your account and the translation license. There is no shared app connection yet. For immediate imports without a key, choose WEB or KJV.

Can I import ESV?

ESV text can currently be entered manually. API.Bible does not offer ESV, and a separate Crossway import connection is not yet included. See API.Bible’s translation guidance for current availability.

Are topics assigned automatically?

No. Grace, Truth, Mercy, and the other starter topics are choices for you to select. Only the topics you choose are saved on an entry. Importing a passage does not analyze it or add topics.

How do I review or edit topics?

Open Review → Manage topics or Settings → Topics. Tap a topic to see its entries, use the plus to add one, or open its menu to rename, merge, or remove it. Renaming updates matching tags in that profile; removing a topic keeps your writing. To merge, rename it to an existing topic.

How do I find an older reflection?

Use Review to search verse references, Scripture, thoughts, prayers, and topics. Combine the activity, topic, date, and favorites filters. Topic chips show entry counts. Change the entry order in Settings or with the sort button.

How do I practice a memory verse?

Open a memory entry and tap Practice this verse. Hide or reveal the passage, write what you remember, and record the practice. An entry’s menu can also copy a Bible reading into Memory Verses with its selected topics.

How do I record an answered prayer?

Open the prayer and tap Record an answer, add your answer notes and the date, then save. You can also edit the prayer’s status to Praying, Answered, or Archived and filter by status in Review.

Does my writing sync between devices?

The current version saves writing on the device and does not include iCloud sync. Saved entries work offline. Use Settings → Export this profile’s journal to save a JSON backup to Files. An import/restore feature is not currently included.

What happens when I remove a profile or activity?

Turning off an activity keeps its entries available in Review. Deleting a profile removes its writing and Bible connection from that device and requires typing its name to confirm. Export any writing you want to keep before deleting a profile. Profiles organize entries but do not lock them with a password.
