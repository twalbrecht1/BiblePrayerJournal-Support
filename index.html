Common Questions

Using your journal

Version 1.2 imports NIV, NLT, and NASB 1995 through the app’s shared service. Choose your Bible version and import a passage—no provider account or API key is needed. Update the app if you still see the earlier connection screen.

How do I get started?

From version 1.1, first let iCloud check for an existing profile from your other device. If one arrives, it opens automatically. If this is your first journal, create a profile with your name, choose a Bible version, and select your activities. You can also choose Create a new profile while iCloud is checking if you want a separate space. Profiles do not require an account or password. Use Settings → Edit name & activities to change your choices, or Add profile to create another space.

Can I turn journal areas off without losing history?

Yes. Open Settings → Journal areas and use the Bible Journal, Prayer Journal, and Memory Verses switches. Turning an area off hides its shortcuts, entries, search results, and history from Today, Journal, Review, and topic browsing. Your writing, favorites, prayer answers, and memory practice history stay saved; turn the area back on to see them again. All three may be off at once. These choices apply to the current profile and sync with its settings. Journal exports include the saved entries from areas that are off.

Can I hide Verse of the Day separately?

Yes. Open Settings → Today and turn Verse of the Day on or off. This only shows or hides the daily passage on Today; your journal areas and saved entries stay unchanged. The choice belongs to the current profile and syncs with its settings.

Can I add more than one reading each day?

Yes. On Journal, select Daily Bible Journal and tap New entry. When only one journal area is enabled, its entries open automatically and the Activity selector is hidden. With multiple areas and All entries selected, choose a Bible reading, prayer, or memory verse from the New entry menu. You can also tap the plus beside Daily Bible Journal on Today. Each entry can have its own passage, verse text, thoughts, topics, and date. Change the date when recording an earlier reading.

How do I import Bible verses?

Enter a passage such as John 3:16–17 or Psalm 23, select a version, and tap Import verses. WEB and KJV work without an API account. Version 1.2 also supports NIV, NLT, and NASB 1995 through the app’s shared service, with no provider account or API key needed by users. Import one chapter or a same-chapter verse range, up to 100 verses. Imports require an internet connection; you can also enter verse text manually.

Do I need an API.Bible account or key?

No. Version 1.2 removes the personal API-key field and account signup link. The app owner manages one shared API.Bible connection for NIV, NLT, and NASB 1995; users only choose a version. These imports are live. If you still see Bible Connection asking for a key, update the app to version 1.2. WEB and KJV work without a key in either version.

Will imported passages and my notes work offline?

Your own reflections, prayers, topics, and memory practice history remain saved and available offline. In version 1.2, new licensed imports save references and editions with your writing. NIV, NLT, and NASB text uses a temporary device cache, refreshed within 30 days; an expired or uncached passage needs an internet connection. Sharing and journal exports include your writing and licensed references rather than copies of licensed Bible text. Manually entered and public-domain text keeps its previous behavior.

What happens if the free Bible allowance is reached?

The shared connection uses API.Bible’s free, noncommercial Starter allowance across the app. If the allowance is reached or a licensed translation is unavailable, the app explains the problem and keeps your draft. WEB, KJV, and manual entry remain available. A requested translation is never silently replaced with another version.

Can I import ESV?

ESV text can currently be entered manually. API.Bible does not offer ESV, and a separate Crossway import connection is not yet included. See API.Bible’s translation guidance for current availability.

Are topics assigned automatically?

No. Grace, Truth, Mercy, and the other starter topics are choices for you to select. Only the topics you choose are saved on an entry. Importing a passage does not analyze it or add topics.

How do I review or edit topics?

Open Review → Manage topics or Settings → Topics. Tap a topic to see its entries, use the plus to add one, or open its menu to rename, merge, or remove it. Renaming updates matching tags in that profile; removing a topic keeps your writing. To merge, rename it to an existing topic.

How do I find an older reflection?

Use Review to search verse references, manually entered or public-domain Scripture, thoughts, prayers, and topics. Licensed text displayed from the temporary cache is not part of the permanent journal search index. Combine the activity, topic, date, and favorites filters. Topic chips show entry counts. Change the entry order in Settings or with the sort button.

How do I practice a memory verse?

Open a memory entry and tap Practice this verse. Hide or reveal the passage, write what you remember, and record the practice. For a version 1.2 licensed import, load the passage online or from its unexpired device cache before recording the practice. An entry’s menu can also copy a Bible reading into Memory Verses with its selected topics.

How do I record an answered prayer?

Open the prayer and tap Record an answer, add your answer notes and the date, then save. You can also edit the prayer’s status to Praying, Answered, or Archived and filter by status in Review.

Does my writing sync between devices?

From version 1.1, the app syncs profiles, topics, settings, readings, prayers, and memory verses through your private iCloud account. Install the updated app on both devices, use the same Apple Account with iCloud enabled, and keep both apps open and connected for the first transfer. Settings → iCloud sync shows the account state and successful upload/download times. Saved entries still work offline. Use Settings → Export this profile’s journal to save a JSON backup to Files. An import/restore feature is not currently included.

Why is my iPhone profile missing on iPad?

Earlier versions stored writing only on each device. Update both devices to the latest version without uninstalling the iPhone app. Check that both use the same Apple Account and have iCloud enabled, then allow time for the iPhone to upload and the iPad to download. Use the same app release on both devices. If an account or transfer fails, the app shows the problem instead of assuming your journal is empty.

What if I already created a second profile on iPad?

Both profiles are kept, even if their names match. After syncing, use Settings → Profile management to select the original iPhone profile. Profiles are identified separately and are not merged by name. Keep any writing in the other profile until you have saved or copied what you need.

What happens when I delete a profile?

Deleting a profile removes its writing from this device and iCloud, including entries in areas that are off and other synced devices once they connect, and requires typing its name to confirm. For older versions, the app also attempts to remove an unused personal API key stored on the device where you delete the profile. Export any writing you want to keep before deleting a profile. Profiles organize entries but do not lock them with a password.
