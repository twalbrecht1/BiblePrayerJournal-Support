Support Privacy

Last updated October 5, 2026

Privacy Policy

Your journal writing stays on your device. Scripture imports connect to outside providers as described below.

Information you enter

The app stores the information you choose to enter, including profile names, activity preferences, Bible versions, journal entries, passage references, verse text, reflections, prayer requests and answers, dates, topics, favorites, and memory verse practice history.

Local storage and profiles

Profiles and entries are stored locally on the device. The current version does not include iCloud sync, server accounts, or an online journal database. The developer does not receive your profile names, reflections, or prayers through the app. Profiles separate writing within the app but are not password protected; anyone with access to the app on your unlocked device may access them.

Scripture imports

When you import a passage or load Verse of the Day, the selected passage reference and translation are sent to a Scripture provider. WEB and KJV use bible-api.com. Configured licensed imports use API.Bible and send the API key needed to authorize the request. These providers may receive connection information such as your IP address and handle it under their own policies. Your thoughts, prayer text, profile names, and topic selections are not included in these requests.

API keys and Scripture usage reports

API.Bible keys are stored per profile in this device’s iOS Keychain. They are not included in journal exports or embedded in the app. When API.Bible content is displayed, the app sends the provider its Scripture usage tokens with randomly generated installation and session identifiers. These reports do not contain your name or journal writing. See API.Bible’s usage reporting guidance.

Advertising and analytics

The current app does not include advertising or a separate general-purpose analytics service. The Scripture usage reports described above support the Bible provider’s content usage requirements. The app does not sell your journal information.

Exporting and sharing

If you export a journal or share an entry, you choose its destination using the system’s Files or sharing controls. An export contains the profile’s writing, topics, settings, and entry metadata, and excludes API keys. Exported files and shared copies are handled by the locations and services you choose. Deleting an entry in the app does not delete copies you have exported or shared.

Support and this website

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages and does not add analytics scripts, tracking cookies, or a contact form that collects your journal data. GitHub may process website request information under its privacy statement. Support and feedback submitted through GitHub Issues are processed by GitHub and are publicly visible. Do not include private journal writing, personal prayer requests, names, or API keys in a submission.

Retention and deletion

Entries remain on the device until you delete them or remove the app’s local data. Deleting a profile removes that profile’s entries and attempts to remove its stored API key. You can explicitly remove a key in Settings → Bible Connection → Remove connection. Keychain credentials can remain after uninstalling an app, so remove the connection before uninstalling if you want to remove those credentials as well. Keep a backup of writing you want to retain.

Policy changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the app’s features or data practices change. The latest revision date appears above. For questions, visit the support page or open a support request using a general description without private information.
